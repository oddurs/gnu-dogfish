What a distribution needs before shipping Dogfish as the default: full
locale behaviour, the remaining GNU grammar and precision gaps closed,
translated messages, man pages and completions, a verifiable supply chain,
performance tracked against GNU, and a clear answer to how Dogfish relates
to uutils/coreutils.

## Release gate

- The GNU test-suite pass rate is at parity with the best Rust implementation,
  with every remaining failure tracked as an item.
- Every item in this milestone is done or explicitly dropped with its reasoning.
