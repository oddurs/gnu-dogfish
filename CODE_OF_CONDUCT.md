# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the spirit of Free Software and hacker camaraderie, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **GNU/Dogfish** a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Focusing on technical excellence, user freedom, and constructive code review.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them navigate systems programming in Rust.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and benchmark-driven feedback.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the repository maintainer (`@oddurs`). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
